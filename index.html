Terms and Conditions

1. The registration fee of KSh 200 is a one-time fee and is non-refundable once processing has begun.

2. Placement in a work crew is subject to the availability of slots in the applicant's area. Registration does not guarantee immediate placement.

3. Daily rates range from KSh 1,000 to KSh 1,500 depending on the role and location of assignment.

4. Payments are made weekly via M-Pesa to the registered phone number.

5. Applicants must be 18 years of age or older and hold a valid National Identity Card.
